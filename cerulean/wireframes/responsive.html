BLU by TITAN: desktop and tablet wireframes

Half-scale sketches: the desktop frame stands for a 1280px-wide screen, the tablet frame for 768px. Pale blue strips are the side margins that grow as the screen widens. Content sits in a centred column (about 1120px at most); only the hero photo band and the dark statement band run edge to edge. The phone layout does not change. Numbers match the notes.

The one real choice: "A day with BLU" on desktop

On a phone the glucose line runs down the page. On a wide screen it can turn sideways, so time runs left to right like a real glucose chart.
